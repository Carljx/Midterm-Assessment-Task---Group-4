LIVE MORE SUSTAINABLY

Simple Eco Tips

Small changes in your everyday routine can help protect nature, reduce waste, and create a healthier planet.

TIP 01

Use Less Energy

Turn off lights, fans, and electronics when you leave a room. Choosing energy-efficient appliances can also reduce energy use.

TIP 02

Reduce and Reuse

Choose reusable bottles, bags, containers, and cups instead of disposable products whenever possible.

TIP 03

Save Water

Take shorter showers, fix leaking faucets, and turn off the tap while brushing your teeth.

TIP 04

Protect Green Spaces

Care for trees, gardens, parks, and other natural spaces in your community. Avoid damaging plants and wildlife habitats.

TIP 05

Travel Greener

Walk, cycle, carpool, or use public transportation when practical. Fewer car trips can help reduce emissions.

TIP 06

Choose Sustainable

Support environmentally responsible products and businesses, and choose durable items that you can use for a long time.

One Habit at a Time

You don't have to change everything at once. Start with one simple habit and build from there.
